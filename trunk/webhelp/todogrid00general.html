Overview

The "ToDo Grid" expert offers ...

convenient ways to create, handle and use bookmarks. The expert has a dockable main window; this mode should also be the primary form of use. The expert complements the bookmark function available in the IDE and offers new ways to keep a good overview of the bookmarks set and to access them specifically.

Highlight rows

Double-clicking on a fixed column in a row colors the entire row in a configurable color. The status isn't saved beyond runtime and will be overwritten by a scan - nevertheless, the functionality is very useful for temporarily marking any selection of rows.

Notice

▪

While the highlight filter is active, the row status cannot be changed.

 

Sort grid

All columns of the display grid, with the exception of the row index column, can be sorted individually by clicking in the right area of the fixed cell in the top row. If the column is currently sorted, a triangle symbol appears there; the orientation of the triangle indicates the sorting direction.

Notice

▪

Typically, sorting is performed across multiple columns.
